Tools

Technology

Five tools I use to give you more accurate feedback than the eye alone can offer.

Coach Now

A coaching platform so all communication, notes, videos and lesson feedback are in one place.

Vertex Putting Sensor

A motion sensor device that gives realistic and detailed putting data on your stroke.

Hackmotion Golf

3D wrist sensors that record exactly how your hands behave during the golf swing. Showing detail that is not visible to the naked eye for full swings, short game and putting.

Sportsbox Golf

The latest cutting edge 3D swing analysis software. Creating measurable goals for your swing to guarantee the correct areas are worked on.

Flightscope Mevo 2

A launch monitor that gives me the ability to measure key impact variables including club face, club path and strike location. It has enhanced the way I teach, as I can use it to simulate real world conditions — emulating the stresses of a golf course by setting distance and directional parameters, helping the transfer of better technique to the course.
